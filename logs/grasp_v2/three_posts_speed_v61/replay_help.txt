Collect native demonstrations/DAgger corrections and evaluate free BC motors.

positional arguments:
  {collect,train,fit_jaws,fit_router,fit_contact,evaluate,play,repackage}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --output OUTPUT
  --policy POLICY
  --datasets DATASETS [DATASETS ...]
  --num_envs NUM_ENVS
  --episodes EPISODES
  --episode_steps EPISODE_STEPS
  --jitter_mm JITTER_MM
  --yaw_deg YAW_DEG
  --sources SOURCES [SOURCES ...]
  --targets TARGETS [TARGETS ...]
  --paired_posts        Reset-only round-robin pairing: sources[i] to
                        targets[i]
  --gripper_load_guard  Explicit temporary force relief on jaws; neural task
                        decisions unchanged
  --upright_grasp_support
                        Explicit bounded roll/pitch assistance after measured
                        grasp
  --learner_fraction LEARNER_FRACTION
                        DAgger probability of executing the learner per
                        environment/step
  --learner_until_stage LEARNER_UNTIL_STAGE
                        Limit DAgger perturbations to approach stages while
                        collecting initial recoveries
  --learner_prefix_steps LEARNER_PREFIX_STEPS
                        Collection-only alternative: execute a continuous
                        learner prefix, then let the expert recover without
                        alternating controllers
  --sync_learner_reference
                        Collection-only: reanchor expert private motor
                        references during the learner prefix and at handoff
  --receiver_recovery_handoff
                        Collection-only: start expert receiver reach from a
                        measured stable donor lift at the end of the learner
                        prefix
  --updates UPDATES
  --batch_size BATCH_SIZE
  --architecture {mlp,neighbors,gru,pose,mixture,linear_options}
  --future_steps FUTURE_STEPS
                        BC target from future measured poses; no future
                        observations at inference
  --phase_endpoint      For pose BC, label measured stage endpoints offline;
                        stage is NOT an actor input
  --pose_entry_supervision
                        BC only: fit latched option poses from measured entry
                        states; gate and jaws retain current-state inputs
  --exclude_joint_shortcuts
                        BC option gate: use measured geometry/contact; exclude
                        joint and velocity correlations
  --skill {lift,handover,full}
  --balance_direction {-1.0,1.0}
                        Collection-only receiver contact centering experiment
  --receiver_mode {wall,vertex}
                        Data-collection receiver grasp candidate; inference
                        decisions stay in the actor
  --receiver_wall_angle {60.0,300.0}
                        Collection-only upper/lower right wall grasp candidate
                        in degrees
  --receiver_wall_depth RECEIVER_WALL_DEPTH
                        Collection-only wall insertion depth below the upper
                        rim in mm
  --receiver_wall_tilt RECEIVER_WALL_TILT
                        Collection-only wall tool entry tilt in degrees
  --receiver_open_angle RECEIVER_OPEN_ANGLE
                        Collection-only wall jaw opening in radians
  --receiver_release_bottom RECEIVER_RELEASE_BOTTOM
                        Collection-only insertion bottom height in metres
                        before release; success still requires free stable
                        seating at 0.010 m
  --receiver_pressure_min RECEIVER_PRESSURE_MIN
                        Collection-only lower receiver pressure in N
  --receiver_pressure_max RECEIVER_PRESSURE_MAX
                        Collection-only upper receiver pressure in N
  --donor_release_rate DONOR_RELEASE_RATE
                        Collection-only gradual donor release rate in rad/s
  --fixed_donor_during_contact
                        Collection-only experiment: preserve donor tool pose
                        during receiver approach and closure
  --recapture_receiver_after_transfer
                        Collection-only: measure the loaded receiver-to-peg
                        transform after the donor retreats
  --implementation_change IMPLEMENTATION_CHANGE
                        Description of the current-code fork when repackaging
                        unchanged weights
  --transition_data TRANSITION_DATA [TRANSITION_DATA ...]
                        Repackage: infer option-transition memory from
                        complete training demonstrations
  --stationary_data STATIONARY_DATA [STATIONARY_DATA ...]
                        Repackage: infer which tool references remain
                        stationary from whole training demonstrations
  --stationary_position_tolerance_mm STATIONARY_POSITION_TOLERANCE_MM
                        Training reference excursions below this limit share
                        the previous neural goal
  --stationary_orientation_tolerance_rad STATIONARY_ORIENTATION_TOLERANCE_RAD
                        Training rotation excursions below this limit share
                        the previous neural orientation
  --pickup_policy PICKUP_POLICY
                        Repackage: embed a preserved six-option neural pickup
                        decoder in a full actor
  --use_pickup_gate     Repackage: retain the embedded pickup neural decisions
                        until the full learned router selects continuation
  --track_stationary_adjustments
                        Repackage: retain the entry anchor and follow bounded
                        changes in learned contact pose predictions
  --relative_pose_data RELATIVE_POSE_DATA [RELATIVE_POSE_DATA ...]
                        Repackage: fit a peg-frame BC goal decoder from whole
                        native training visits
  --relative_pose_options RELATIVE_POSE_OPTIONS [RELATIVE_POSE_OPTIONS ...]
                        Options whose receiver poses are fitted in the
                        observed peg frame
  --router_jaw_inputs   Router fitting: include measured jaw
                        positions/references to distinguish donor release and
                        retreat
  --target_object_data TARGET_OBJECT_DATA [TARGET_OBJECT_DATA ...]
                        Repackage: fit object destinations from complete
                        training endpoints; decode through measured receiver
                        grasp
  --target_object_options TARGET_OBJECT_OPTIONS [TARGET_OBJECT_OPTIONS ...]
                        Options whose object destinations are fitted relative
                        to the target
  --track_relative_pose_changes
                        Repackage: follow bounded changes of fitted peg-
                        relative goals after entry
  --diagnostic_events   Log all eligible environments at learned option
                        changes and every 50 steps; no actor input changes
  --diagnostic_interval DIAGNOSTIC_INTERVAL
                        Diagnostic-only physical sampling interval in control
                        steps
  --grasp_support GRASP_SUPPORT
                        Explicit bounded grasp spring strength 0..1; all
                        nonzero runs are assisted
  --support_profile {gentle,firm}
                        Assisted grasp force/stiffness preset; strength zero
                        remains native
  --placement_support   Explicit temporary centering guide after free release
                        near the target; assisted only
  --free_speed_scale FREE_SPEED_SCALE
                        Reference translation/rotation multiplier away from
                        contact; original checkpoint uses 1
  --contact_speed_scale CONTACT_SPEED_SCALE
                        Reference translation/rotation multiplier in contact;
                        jaws and hold times stay unchanged
  --motion_profile {contact,clearance,travel,fast_travel}
                        Experimental transit caps; travel preserves original
                        orientation and approach minima
  --transport_speed_scale TRANSPORT_SPEED_SCALE
                        Clearance profile only: stable isolated grasp
                        transport multiplier, 1..4; interaction stays slow
  --latch_option_poses  Repackage: choose and retain a learned pose on each
                        learned option change; jaws remain closed-loop learned
                        outputs
  --pressure_supervision
                        Fit jaws: imitate offline expert pressure decisions
                        with categorical rates and force calibration
  --pressure_min PRESSURE_MIN
                        Training-only lower pressure label boundary in N
  --pressure_max PRESSURE_MAX
                        Training-only upper pressure label boundary in N
  --seed SEED
  --device DEVICE
  --headless
  --realtime
  --video
